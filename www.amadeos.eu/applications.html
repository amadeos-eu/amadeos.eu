Applications for every day

amadeOS brings its own applications, all written in Java and all following the desktop's colours, fonts and language. LibreOffice and Firefox are there for everything else.

File Manager

Folders and files in a tree, in one or two panels, with drag and drop.

WritePad

Letters and documents on real pages; opens and saves ODT and Word files.

Mailer

E-mail from one or more accounts over IMAP and SMTP, with address completion from Contacts.

Calendar

Appointments, reminders, To Do items and birthdays, stored as iCalendar.

Contacts

Addresses, phone numbers and birthdays, stored as vCard; imports from other programs.

Text Editor

Notes, lists and configuration files.

Books

E-books (EPUB) and comics (CBZ) on a bookshelf of your own, with fifteen classics included.

PDF Viewer

Read, search and print PDF documents.

Image Viewer

Photos and pictures, slideshows, camera data and maps.

Audio Station

Audio CDs, music files and internet radio.

Video Player

Video files, streams and DVDs.

Image Scan

Scan photos and letters, from USB and network scanners.

Calculator

Basic, scientific and programmer modes.

Fractals

Explore the Mandelbrot set.

Clipboard

A history of what you copied, to paste it again.

Print Manager

Printers and print jobs.

Session Diagnostics

The logs of every program, in one window.

System Information

What is inside your computer, and what amadeOS knows about it.

And everything else

LibreOffice Writer, Calc, Impress, Draw and Math for office work, and Firefox for the web, with telemetry, studies and sponsored content turned off and uBlock Origin installed. The Software program installs any of the thousands of programs that Debian offers.

Every amade application is a Debian package of its own, written in Java with Swing. The Application Developer's Handbook explains how they are made, one program per chapter.
