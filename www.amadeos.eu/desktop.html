A calm, modern desktop

The amadeOS desktop, Horizon, has a top bar, a dock, a search and widgets on the desktop. Display manager, window manager, panels and Settings are new programs written in Java.

Horizon

A top bar, a dock and flat windows with rounded corners, in a dark or light scheme and six accent colours.

Everything in its place

Switching windows, one place for all settings, the login screen, installing programs and amadeOS itself.

Bonus: the Classic look

Who knows the Common Desktop Environment of the 1990s can have it back: Front Panel, Motif frames and the original backdrops. See the Classic look.

Under the hood

The desktop consists of a few Debian packages: the display manager with the login screen, the session with screen lock and screen saver, a reparenting X11 window manager, the Horizon top bar and dock, and Settings. The window manager speaks the ICCCM and EWMH standards, so programs that are not written in Java work as well. Part VII of the User's Handbook explains how it all fits together, and What amadeOS adds to Debian gives an overview.
