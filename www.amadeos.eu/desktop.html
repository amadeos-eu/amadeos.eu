Two looks, one desktop

amadeOS comes with two looks of the same desktop. Horizon, the default, has a top bar, a dock, search and widgets. Classic follows the Common Desktop Environment of the 1990s, with the Front Panel and Motif frames. You switch in Settings or at login, and the windows, the panel and the programs follow right away. Display manager, window manager, panels and Settings are new programs in Java.

Horizon

The look new accounts start with: a dark or light scheme, six accent colours and flat icons.

Classic

The look of CDE, made new: the Front Panel with drawers, four named workspaces and Motif window frames.

Under the hood

The desktop consists of a few Debian packages: the display manager with the login screen, the session with screen lock and screen saver, a reparenting X11 window manager, the Horizon top bar and dock or the Front Panel, and Settings. The window manager speaks the ICCCM and EWMH standards, so programs that are not written in Java work as well. Part VII of the User's Handbook explains how it all fits together, and What amadeOS adds to Debian gives an overview.
