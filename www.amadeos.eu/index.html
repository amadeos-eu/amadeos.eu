Digital sovereignty starts on your desktop.

amadeOS is a free, European desktop operating system. Debian and Linux underneath, and on top a desktop written entirely in Java, in the modern Horizon look, with its own applications and games. Open source from the login screen to the text editor, under the EU's own licence.

Get amadeOS Why amadeOS?

Free, open and European

Everything in amadeOS is free software under the EUPL-1.2, a licence published by the European Commission. No account, no telemetry, no advertising: your computer works for you.

A calm, modern desktop

Horizon: top bar, dock, search and widgets, dark or light, in six accent colours.

Applications for every day

Files, documents, mail, calendar, contacts, books, pictures and music, plus LibreOffice and Firefox.

Twenty games

Mines and Solitaire on board; strategy, city building, an adventure, the 3D shooter Rivet and more one click away.

Three handbooks

For users, for application developers and for game makers, online and on every amadeOS computer.
