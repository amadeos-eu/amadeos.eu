amadeOS in brief

amadeOS is a free, European desktop operating system. It is based on Debian 13 “trixie” and brings a desktop of its own that is written entirely in Java (Swing on Xorg): login screen, window manager, the Horizon desktop with top bar, dock, search and widgets, Settings and a software centre. On top come 30 own programs, among them Files, WritePad, Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Books, Calls, TV and Backup & Sync, and 18 games on an own game engine. Everything is free software under the European Union Public Licence (EUPL-1.2). There is no account, no telemetry and no advertising, and updates come from a signed apt repository.

Kurzbeschreibung auf Deutsch

amadeOS ist ein freies, europäisches Desktop-Betriebssystem auf Basis von Debian 13 „trixie“. Der gesamte Desktop ist in Java geschrieben (Swing auf Xorg): Anmeldebildschirm, Fenstermanager, die Oberfläche Horizon mit oberer Leiste, Dock, Suche und Widgets, Einstellungen und Software-Center. Dazu kommen 30 eigene Programme, darunter Dateien, Textverarbeitung, Mail, Kalender, Kontakte, E-Book-Reader, Videoanrufe, TV und Datensicherung, sowie 18 Spiele auf einer eigenen Spiele-Engine. Facts

  • Current version: 1.2.0, released on 7 November 2026 (release notes). First release: 1.0.0 on 25 October 2026.
  • New in 1.2.0: Calls (end-to-end encrypted video calls in the local network, with chat, files and screen sharing), the Font Viewer, the new logo, login and lock screens in the Horizon look, the Lava Lamp screen saver, and new programs that arrive with the normal updates.
  • Base: Debian 13 “trixie”, unchanged; Linux, Xorg/X11, systemd, apt, Firefox and LibreOffice come from Debian.
  • Desktop: written in Java 25 and Swing; Horizon look (dark or light, six accent colours), Classic look in the style of CDE as a bonus.
  • Programs and games: 30 own programs and 18 games, from Mines and Solitaire to a city builder, an adventure and a 3D shooter.
  • Download: one ISO image for 64-bit PCs (1.9 GB), BIOS and UEFI, live system with installer. The desktop can also be installed as packages on Debian 13 or Ubuntu 26.04 from apt.amadeos.eu. Download page.
  • Licence: EUPL-1.2.
  • Documentation: three handbooks for users, application developers and game developers at docs.amadeos.eu, also installed on every amadeOS computer.
  • Source code: github.com/amadeos-eu.
  • Who: designed and maintained by Ralf Eichinger (datazuul, Munich, Germany). Much of the code was written together with Claude, an AI coding assistant by Anthropic; every change is reviewed, tested and merged by the maintainer.
